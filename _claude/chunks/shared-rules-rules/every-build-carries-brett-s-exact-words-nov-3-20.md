## Before building anything

- **Every build carries Brett's exact words (Nov 3 2026, Brett: "All the build entries, like wherever it shows a build in the build manager, pending, all that, I need it to show the prompt, what I said, the verbatim prompt that kicked it off, that initiated it."):** every build launch includes Brett's exact words, verbatim and unedited, in `origin.said` / the brief's `## Brett said (verbatim)` block (optional meta line `Source: voice · At: <iso> · Session: <id> · Link: <url>`, then his words as a `> ` quote); a later change he asks for goes in as `## Addendum N (Brett, <date> (<source>))`. A paraphrase goes in the brief, never in place of his words. Launchers record it on the build (`deploy/claude-headless.sh` → `framework/cli/build-origin.mjs capture`; orchestrator `add` / MCP `pending_change add` / Plan Studio *Turn into a build* take `said`), and every view of a build shows it first ("What Brett said"). Commands: `node framework/cli/build-origin.mjs get|set|addendum|missing|block` (REST/MCP through the build-orchestrator plugin's `/origin` routes).
