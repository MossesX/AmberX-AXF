## Before building anything

- **Record every session (Nov 1 2026, Brett: "all our sessions get recorded to axHub"; automatic since Nov 6 2026, Brett: "automatically audio built in transcriptions automatic every session"):** a session that calls `claude_session_start` needs nothing more: the console asks the recorder for it and every part is transcribed on the VPS (Settings → Plugins → Claude → Session recording). Any other Claude session (claude.ai chat, voice, Claude Code; any machine) calls axHub `amberx.recorder_start` at its start with `session_name` (the thread/task name; optional `group`, `note`), then keeps working: it is a **straight approval**, not a veto (axHub decision #108), so nothing records until Brett taps **Approve** on axHub, and Android then asks for its own capture consent on the phone. Send it once, don't poll or re-send. At the end of the session call `amberx.recorder_stop` (runs directly). `amberx.recorder_status` (runs directly) shows on/off, since, the current file, uploaded parts, the queue and whether the phone is online. AmberX's existing Audio Output Recorder records the phone's playback only (never the microphone), in 30-minute parts named `<session_name> - <YYYY-MM-DD HH-mm> - partN`, grouped under `session_name` on axHub. Phone offline = a clear `unavailable` error: say so in one line and carry on; headless builds with nobody at the phone skip it.
