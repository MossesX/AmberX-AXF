## Before building anything

- **Read Claude settings first, and follow them (Nov 3 2026, Brett: "A whole bunch of settings that are for Claude, that you check on axHub and then act accordingly."):** at session start and before acting, every Claude session (voice, chat, Claude Code, headless builds, hired and swarm agents) reads the Claude settings on axHub and follows them: MCP `claude_settings_get` (AmberX-AXF console, read-only), or `node apps/console-web/plugins/claude-settings/claude-settings.mjs get` in an AmberX-AXF checkout, or (claude.ai) `axhub.memory_read` project `shared`, area `data`, path `claude/settings.json` (missing keys = their defaults). They cover No wait, Follow Amber, builds (default model, launch without asking, max parallel, per-build and daily budget caps: stop and notify when hit), verification, talking (persona, tone, swearing, length, costs), notifications and memory. A session may change the others (`claude_settings_set`, veto); **owner-only** ones (No wait, Include irreversible, launch without asking, default model, UI Review gate, raising a cap) change only with Brett's own click in the console (Settings → Plugins → Claude), never from a session, an agent or a tool. No wait ON = a standing veto pass Brett's click created: held calls run at once until it is switched off (or ends, or night comes). <!-- axf-plugin:claude-settings -->
