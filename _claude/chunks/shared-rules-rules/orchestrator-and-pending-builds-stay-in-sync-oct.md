## Before building anything

- **Orchestrator and Pending builds stay in sync (Oct 27 2026, Brett; one store since Nov 1 2026):** "All builds go to the orchestrator, which then splits everything into steps. It sorts the steps into Opus and Fable and sends out the list to the pending build and the build manager. They need to all stay in sync." They can't drift any more: every build of every profile is one record in the builds store (`_claude/builds/store.json`, builds@1, owned by the orchestrator), and the orchestrator view, the Pending builds list (steps as `[Fable]`/`[Opus]` sub prompts), the build queue and the Build manager are projections of it. Every writer (`orchestrator.mjs`, `pending.mjs`, `build-queue.mjs`, the console, MCP `pending_change`, plugins) writes the store under one lock and every write is versioned in `_claude/builds/journal/`; the old orchestrator/pending/build-queue files are read-only exports. A session without a shell (claude.ai) changes builds through the orchestrator (console or MCP), never by writing an export through `axhub.memory_write`; a direct edit of an export is only taken in when it is newer. Checks: `node framework/cli/builds-store.mjs verify`, `node framework/spec/check-builds-store.mjs`. (The `orch-sync.mjs` bridge this rule used to name was retired with the store.) <!-- axf-plugin:build-orchestrator -->
