## Before building anything

- **Model upgrades need Brett; downgrades don't (Nov 3 2026, Brett: "It cannot be switching the model without my approval. That's not even a veto. That's an approval." … "You can downgrade, but you can't upgrade."; after a headless build approved its own phase switch because its brief said "do every step"):** a build may switch to a **lower** model on its own (e.g. Fable → Opus): allowed, no approval, just recorded. Switching to a **higher** model (e.g. Opus → Fable) needs Brett's explicit **approval** (confirm, never a veto countdown): his own signed-in console click or an axHub approval he approves. No session, agent, headless build or the orchestrator ever approves an upgrade, and nobody launches or relaunches a build on a higher model than its step. A session that reaches an upgrade stops there and asks (`orchestrator.mjs request-upgrade <id> <phase>`). The ranking is one ordered list in `framework/core/model-policy.mjs` (Fable above Opus; a new model slots in there). Enforced in the orchestrator, the console, the launcher (`framework/cli/model-gate.mjs`) and the swarm planner (`framework/core/model-switch.mjs`). <!-- axf-plugin:build-orchestrator -->
