## Oct 20 2026 — App catalog on axHub (app-catalog@1)

- Oct 20 2026: **themes come from axHub.** The developer apps that are console tabs (App tabs plugin: Console, Pilot, axHub, and any app added as a tab) load their theme selection (colour, style, layout, spacing … the appearance slots) from axHub — not every AXF app: a new app uses its own theme unless it's added as a console tab; when axHub is unreachable it renders the framework default theme. In the console, a theme change made in any app tab (Console, Pilot, axHub …) is saved to axHub and every tab/app follows it live.
