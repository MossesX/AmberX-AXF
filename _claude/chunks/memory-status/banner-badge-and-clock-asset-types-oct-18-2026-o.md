## Status (as of Oct 21 2026)

- **Banner, badge and clock asset types (Oct 18 2026 on Opus; Fable rebuild Oct 20 2026, report `_claude/memory/docs/rebuilds/banner-assets.md`):** three permanent framework types in `asset-types.json` (`"optional": true`, so older apps without them still validate). **banner** (hideable chrome): thin permanent bar above the layout (`.fx-frame` wraps the shell), `height` (16–48, thin 24) → `--fx-banner-h`, `surface` flat/raised/accent (unknown → flat), `logo {show,text}`, `divider`, and `attach: [{type,id,at: left|center|right,order?}]`: it **hosts other assets**. **badge** and **clock** are `"attachable": true`: placed on a host, never picked in Appearance or rolled. Engine `core/engines/banner.ts` = Kotlin `engine/BannerEngine.kt` (`placeAttachments`/`place` through the widget host engine, `bannerVars`/`bannerHeight`, `bannerSurface`, `formatClock`, `clockTickMs`, tone tokens); fixtures `spec/fixtures/banner.json` unchanged. What a banner takes comes from `asset-types.json` → `banner.attach` (one definition for the engines and all three validators, which now reject a bad `attach` entry at build time). Runtime (both ports): `assetOrNull`, `attachToBanner({type,id,at,order,origin})` (same type+id replaces; a bad type/side/missing asset warns in the terminal, channel `widgets`), `detachFromBanner(key|origin)`, `detachAll(origin)`, `banner()`, `showBannerPopup(key,text,ms)` + `dismissBannerPopup()` (state `bannerPopup`). React `components/Banner.tsx` (clock ticks on the minute, tap closes a popup). **Android Compose banner** `axf-ui/components/Banner.kt` (`AxfBanner` above the grid in `AppShell`, `AxfBannerBadge`, popup, `LocalBannerForeground`), shown in the Android console (starters bundled). Starters: `banner/amberx-thin` (default), `banner/amberx-accent`, `clock/digital-12h|24h`, `badge/new`. The console detaches a disabled plugin's items from every host. CI: `framework/spec/check-widgets.mts` (web) + Kotlin `ConformanceTest`/`KernelTest` (written without Maven; a local kotlinc harness replayed the fixtures).
